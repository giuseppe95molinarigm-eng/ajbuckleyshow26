# Photography: generation prompts

No image generator was available when this sample was built, so the four frames in the PDF are labelled **"Photograph to come"**.
Save each final image as `source/images/<slug>.jpg` and run the build again. The build places the photo automatically with `object-fit: cover`.

**Frame:** 286.5 × 270 pt (3.98 × 3.75 in), ratio about 1.06 : 1.
**Minimum size:** 1200 × 1125 px for 300 ppi effective. Generate square at 2048 px or larger. The layout crops about 3% from the top and bottom, so keep the dish centred with breathing room on all sides.

## Shared style block (append to every prompt)

> Photorealistic editorial food photograph for a premium holiday cookbook. Soft natural window light from the left, gentle shadows, warm but true colour, slightly lifted blacks, fine natural grain. Home kitchen styling with small realistic imperfections: a few crumbs, an uneven edge, a slightly creased linen napkin. Restrained Christmas mood: warm ivory and dark wood surfaces, one deep fir-green or bordeaux linen napkin, at most one out-of-focus fir sprig at the far edge of the frame and never touching the food. Shallow depth of field, 45-degree camera angle unless stated, 50–85 mm lens look. Square composition, dish centred with generous margins. No text, no labels, no logos, no watermark, no collage, no hands, no glossy plastic look, no added garnish or ingredients beyond those described.
